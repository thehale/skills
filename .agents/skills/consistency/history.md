- An extension point lands together with its first use, or in a preparatory
  commit just before it. Never add one ahead of time.
- A refactor gets its own commit, separate from the feature it makes easier.
- When a principle comes up in review, apply it everywhere it reaches, not
  just to the line it was raised on.
